01 / Overview

Curation Media's products are built on intellectual property developed over eight years of engineering by Duration Media. The patent portfolio covers real-time viewability detection, incremental impression generation, attention-validated supply curation, and sustainability-aligned bid stream optimization.

This page provides public notice of the patents that cover Curation Media's products and services, including Refresh Detect™ and VaaS® (Viewability-as-a-Service®). The portfolio includes both issued patents and pending applications. Listings are illustrative and may not be exhaustive.

02 / Patent portfolio

The following patents and pending applications cover the core technology underlying Curation Media's products. Each is associated with one or more product lines.

US PATENT — REFRESH DETECT™ Issued

Real-time refresh detection and incremental impression generation for digital advertising

A system and method for detecting when an ad unit on a publisher property has been genuinely viewed and qualifies for an incremental impression — distinct from time-based or scroll-based refresh — using real-time viewability and attention signals.

03 / VaaS® claims

VaaS® (Viewability-as-a-Service®) is covered by issued United States patents. The mark VaaS® is a registered trademark of Curation Media's affiliated entity, Duration Media. Use of the mark and the underlying methodology is restricted to licensed deployments.

  • The phrase "Viewability-as-a-Service" is a registered trademark — and any unauthorized use in commercial advertising-technology contexts is prohibited.
  • The underlying real-time viewability validation methodology is covered by issued patents.
  • Third-party measurement verification is provided by IAS and DoubleVerify under their own respective contracts.

04 / Refresh Detect™

Refresh Detect™ generates incremental, highly viewable ad impressions through a patented method of detecting genuine refresh signals — distinct from naïve time-based or scroll-based refresh techniques.

  • Refresh Detect™ is covered by an issued U.S. patent and one or more pending applications.
  • The mark "Refresh Detect" is used under trademark protection and is exclusive to Curation Media's product line.
  • The technology operates entirely within the publisher's existing ad stack — no proprietary ad server, no proprietary header bidder.
