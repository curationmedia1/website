04 / QUESTIONS

Common questions from buy-side teams.

A few of the most frequent questions we get from media buyers, trading desks, and brand-direct teams.

01
How is your curated inventory different from a PMP?
A PMP is still typically routed through multiple SSPs and resellers. Our inventory is exclusive to Curation Media, generated by Refresh Detect™ on the publisher side, and reached via a single OpenRTB bid request — with no duplication.
02
Do we need a new DSP integration?
No. We integrate via standard OpenRTB with every major DSP. Existing seats and deals can route through us directly. Most buyers are live within a week.
03
How does the sustainability measurement work?
We're aligned to the Ad Net Zero Global Media Sustainability Framework and IAB Tech Lab standards. Carbon-per-impression is calculated using the same methodology your sustainability reporting tools already accept.
04
What kinds of brands buy through Curation Media?
Brands across travel, finance, CPG, automotive, retail, and entertainment — including Disney, Comcast, American Express, Visit Dubai, Marriott, and more than two dozen others.
