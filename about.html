Built by people who think ad-tech

Curation Media is the next chapter of Duration Media — a seven-year journey from real-time viewability intelligence to a full-stack curated SSP. Built bootstrapped. Built for performance. Built to last.

Manifesto

The digital advertising supply chain has been optimized for volume for too long. We believe the next decade belongs to curation — to inventory that is measurably viewable, demonstrably attentive, and accountable to global standards for sustainability. Quality is the only metric that compounds.

01 / HISTORY

Seven years of quiet engineering. One new SSP.

We didn't show up to ad-tech to ride a trend. We built the underlying technology first — then waited for the market to catch up to what we already knew was possible.

2017 / Founded

Duration Media is founded.

With a hypothesis: a viewable impression is worth more than a delivered one, and there's no reason buyers should pay the same for both. Real-time intelligence becomes the foundation.

2018 — 2020 / VaaS®

VaaS® is built and patented.

Viewability-as-a-Service® launches with publishers. Independent measurement by IAS and DoubleVerify validates 90 %+ viewability — a number the open exchange still hasn't matched.

2021 — 2023 / Attention

The shift to attention metrics.

The industry begins measuring attention with Lumen and Adelaide. Duration Media's inventory is verified to outperform the open exchange baseline — independently, repeatably.

2024 / Pivot

The market moves toward direct supply.

Buyers begin actively demanding a shorter, cleaner path to premium inventory — without duplication, reselling, or intermediaries. Our seven-year tech stack is suddenly the right answer.

2025 / Curation Media

Launch of the Curation Media SSP.

Refresh Detect™ goes to market. The SSP launches. Buyers reach unique, curated inventory through a single bid request. Publishers earn incremental, performance-aligned revenue. The full thesis ships.

02 / PRINCIPLES

Four principles that govern every decision.

We're a bootstrapped company. That means we don't take swings we don't believe in — and we don't optimize for anything except the long-term durability of the relationships we build with publishers and advertisers.

01 / PrincipleQuality

Quality is the only compounding metric.

Volume gets re-baselined every quarter. Quality — measured by viewability, attention, and outcomes — accrues into brand equity for the buyer and pricing power for the publisher.

02 / PrincipleDirect

Every intermediary is a tax on quality.

Each reseller hop adds latency, takes margin, and obscures attribution. We removed them. Buyers reach our inventory through a single bid request — full stop.

03 / PrincipleMeasured

If you can't measure it, we don't claim it.

Every metric we publish is verifiable by partners you already trust — IAS, DoubleVerify, Lumen, Adelaide. No proprietary black boxes. No "trust us."

04 / PrincipleAligned

Sustainability is a technical commitment.

We don't sticker our pitch deck with "green." We align to the Ad Net Zero Global Media Sustainability Framework and IAB Tech Lab standards — and we publish the math.

03 / LEADERSHIP

Bootstrapped. Operator-led. Long-term oriented.

Curation Media is led by operators who've built and shipped ad-tech that survived market cycles — not by people optimizing for the next funding round.

04 / STANDARDS

The bodies whose standards we align to.

Curation Media isn't a closed system. Every metric, every methodology, every claim is anchored to standards published by independent industry bodies.

Sustainability

Ad Net Zero Global Media Sustainability Framework

Aligned to the GMSF for measurable, transparent, accountable reduction of carbon emissions in media. Every impression is reported under this methodology.

Technical

IAB Tech Lab standards

Built on OpenRTB and the IAB Tech Lab's specifications for supply path optimization, ads.txt, sellers.json, and brand safety floors.

Verification

Independent measurement

Viewability via IAS and DoubleVerify. Attention via Lumen and Adelaide. We don't measure ourselves — we publish numbers that buyers can verify with their own contracts.
