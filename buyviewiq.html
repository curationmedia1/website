01 / MECHANIC

How BuyViewIQ™ turns your existing inventory into confirmed-viewable inventory.

We don't add ad units. We don't change your site. We measure the impressions your existing stack already delivers — and surface the ones buyers will pay a premium for.

FLOW
01 / Measure

Scan every impression in real time.

Patented real-time intelligence scans your page, identifies every ad unit by GPID, and measures live viewport state at the impression level — not domain-level averages, not historical scores.

02 / Signal

Confirm 90 %+ viewability.

Every impression confirmed 90 %+ in view is flagged as a BuyViewIQ™-certified impression — validated down to the GPID, in real time, before the bid request is sent.

03 / Monetize

Sell into the Highly Viewable PMP.

Certified impressions are packaged into the BuyViewIQ™ Highly Viewable PMP and delivered to buyers through the Curation Media SSP — where they bid with confidence at premium CPMs.

BuyViewIQ™ is a guaranteed CPM uplift. If your CPMs don't increase against your existing baseline on certified impressions, there's no fee. That's the deal.
