About Refresh Detect™

How Refresh Detect™ works

The full technical description — exactly what happens on your page, on every refresh cycle.

Refresh Detect™ is a real-time viewability detection and CPM optimization product built on Curation Media's patented technology (U.S. Patent No. 11,195,210). It operates entirely within a publisher's existing display ad setup via a JavaScript tag deployed through Google Tag Manager or the APS Connections Marketplace — requiring no dev work or changes to the publisher's ad stack.

On every display ad refresh cycle, Refresh Detect™ performs three actions simultaneously: it measures the viewability of the refreshing impression in real time using pixel-level detection, identifies the GPID of the qualifying ad slot, and checks whether Curation Media demand can deliver a CPM that meets or exceeds the publisher's floor by 10% or more. If the ad meets Curation Media's viewability standards and demand can meet that threshold, Refresh Detect™ takes over the refresh and delivers the higher-paying impression. If either condition is not met — the impression is not sufficiently viewable, or Curation Media demand cannot beat the floor — Refresh Detect™ takes no action and the publisher's normal refresh continues untouched.

The result is purely incremental revenue. Publishers never earn less than their existing floor on any impression. All results are independently verified post-delivery by IAS and DoubleVerify, which measure viewability — the higher CPMs are a direct consequence of buying only highly viewable impressions.
